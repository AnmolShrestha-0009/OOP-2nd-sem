C++ streams maintain a state mask using four internal
 state bits—goodbit, eofbit, failbit, and badbit—to track the success or failure of I/O operations.
  The stream status can be checked directly using operator bool, and unrecoverable or 
  logical errors like failbit can be cleared and recovered from using methods such as clear() and ignore().
   More information is available on the source platform.
